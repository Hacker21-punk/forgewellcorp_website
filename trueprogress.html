Introducing TrueProgress

Transforming Aerial Survey Progress Tracking

We are proud to unveil our latest innovation, TrueProgress - an advanced progress-tracking tool that brings unprecedented efficiency and clarity to your construction projects. We understand the challenges you face when dealing with numerous project sites across vast geographical areas.

Designed with your needs in mind, TrueProgress empowers engineering teams, government railway officers, and infrastructure stakeholders to compare aerial shots of sites, assess progress milestone by milestone, and ensure seamless coordination across multiple locations.

  • Instant multi-date survey alignment with chainage and landmark indexing
  • Centralized cloud infrastructure eliminating physical drive shipping
  • Multi-tiered enterprise role permissions with active/inactive survey governance
  • 1-click Excel backup reporting and synchronized visual evidence logs
